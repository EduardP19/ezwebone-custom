How it works

Three steps, and a real person at every one.

Groups are capped at ten people so everyone gets to speak.

STEP 01

Tell us where you are

Your postcode and what you're going through. That's all we ask for at this stage.

STEP 02

We match you to a group

A coordinator calls you within two working days to check the group is the right fit before you turn up.

STEP 03

You meet, weekly

Same room, same faces, same time each week. Come as often as helps. Leave whenever you want.

In a member's words

I'd explained it forty times. In that room I didn't have to explain it once.

Priya, 34 — Watford anxiety group, member since 2024

Every group is facilitated by a trained volunteer who has lived with the same condition. We are not a therapy service and we don't give clinical advice — we sit alongside the care you already have.
